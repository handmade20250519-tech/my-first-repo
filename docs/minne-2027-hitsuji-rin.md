# 2027年干支「未」 正月飾りの代わりに飾るアート 設計書(凛)

## 狙い(STEP3の再回答)
- 飾る人と場所: 30〜40代。マンションの玄関やリビングに、正月飾りの代わりとして置く
- 気分: 凛とした新しさ
- 文字: 絵だけで勝負する。文字は落款風の朱印「未」だけ

※初正月向けの案(A〜J)は `minne-2027-hitsuji-hatsushogatsu.md` に残してある。

## 売り場の考え方(仮説)
- 「干支ポスター」は売れ行きが弱い(ユーザーが確認)。
- 一方で、売れた証拠が取れているのは「正月飾り・干支飾り」の棚(干支の置物の「再販×4」)。
- そこで本作は**アートポスターとしてではなく、「床の間も飾る場所もないマンション向けの、平面の正月飾り」**として出す。商品名とタグの軸は「正月飾り」に置く。

## STEP4 デザイン設計

### コンセプト
**しめ飾りを置けないマンションの玄関で眺める、凛と立つ未の一枚**

### 共通ルール(凛)
| 項目 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| 配色 | 地は冷たく澄んだ白(胡粉白 #F7F5F0)。墨は濃墨 #1E1E1E と薄墨の2段階だけ。朱 #C8553D は朱印を含めて2点まで(丁未の「丁」は火の干支なので、火の色として朱を使う) |
| 余白 | 画面の70%以上。初正月の案よりも空ける |
| 線 | 輪郭をぼかさず、迷いのない一筆で描く。かすれは筆の勢いとして残す |
| 文字 | 右下(または左下)に朱の角印を1つ。印面は篆書風の「未」一字 |
| 飾り方 | 細い黒の額か白木の額を推奨。玄関の正面や靴箱の上に置く想定 |

### 3案
| 案 | 絵柄 | 凛の出し方 |
|---|---|---|
| K | 横を向いて立つ一頭の羊(雄) | 濃墨の一筆で描いた、渦を巻く角。まっすぐ前を見る目 |
| L | 角の渦ひとつだけ | 一筆の渦を円相のように置き、羊の耳と目だけを薄墨で添える。最も抽象的でモダン |
| M | 立つ羊と、朱の水引一本 | 正月飾りの代わりになるよう、あわじ結びの水引を一本だけ描く。朱は水引と朱印の2点 |

#### K 横を向いて立つ羊
```
Create an original, modern Japanese sumi-e art print for the 2027 New Year, the Year of the Sheep (hinoto-hitsuji), to be displayed in a modern apartment entrance instead of traditional New Year decorations.

SUBJECT: A single ram standing still in profile, facing right, head held high and eyes looking straight ahead with quiet dignity. The spiral horn is the focal point: paint it with one confident, continuous stroke of deep black sumi ink, leaving natural dry-brush streaks (kasure) at the end of the stroke. Suggest the body with only a few decisive strokes of lighter grey ink; leave most of the wool as blank paper. Original design only: do NOT depict any existing character or mascot, and do NOT imitate any specific artist's style.

COMPOSITION: Vertical poster. Place the ram in the lower-left area, occupying no more than 25–30% of the canvas. Leave at least 70% of the canvas as empty negative space (Japanese "ma"), with the open space in front of the ram's gaze. Keep all elements at least 8% from every edge for trimming to A-series proportions.

COLOR: Clean, cool off-white paper (#F7F5F0) with a very subtle washi fiber texture. Deep black ink (#1E1E1E) and one lighter grey ink only. The only color is the vermilion seal.

JAPANESE LETTERING: In the lower right, one small square vermilion (#C8553D) artist seal (rakkan), about 4% of the canvas width, with the single kanji 「未」 carved in seal-script (tensho) style, white lines on vermilion, slightly uneven hand-stamped edge. No other text, letters, numbers, signatures or watermarks.

MOOD: Crisp, dignified, fresh, and calm, like the cold clear air of New Year's morning.

OUTPUT: Highest available resolution (4K), 2:3 vertical. Crisp, print-ready flat artwork for an A4/A3 poster. No frame, no mockup, no room scene.
```

#### L 角の渦ひとつ
```
Create an original, minimalist Japanese sumi-e art print for the 2027 New Year, the Year of the Sheep (hinoto-hitsuji), to be displayed in a modern apartment entrance instead of traditional New Year decorations.

SUBJECT: One single bold spiral brushstroke in deep black sumi ink, like a Zen ensō circle that curls inward, forming the spiral horn of a sheep. Next to the spiral, add only a minimal hint of a sheep's face: a small ear and a single calm eye in pale grey ink, so that the spiral clearly reads as a ram's horn. Leave natural dry-brush streaks (kasure) where the stroke ends. Nothing else. Original design only: do NOT depict any existing character or mascot, and do NOT imitate any specific artist's style.

COMPOSITION: Vertical poster. Place the spiral slightly below the vertical center and slightly left of center, about 30% of the canvas width. Leave at least 75% of the canvas as empty negative space (Japanese "ma"). Keep all elements at least 8% from every edge for trimming to A-series proportions.

COLOR: Clean, cool off-white paper (#F7F5F0) with a very subtle washi fiber texture. Deep black ink (#1E1E1E) and one pale grey ink only. The only color is the vermilion seal.

JAPANESE LETTERING: In the lower right, one small square vermilion (#C8553D) artist seal (rakkan), about 4% of the canvas width, with the single kanji 「未」 carved in seal-script (tensho) style, white lines on vermilion, slightly uneven hand-stamped edge. No other text, letters, numbers, signatures or watermarks.

MOOD: Sharp, modern, meditative, and fresh, a clean start to the year.

OUTPUT: Highest available resolution (4K), 2:3 vertical. Crisp, print-ready flat artwork for an A4/A3 poster. No frame, no mockup, no room scene.
```

#### M 立つ羊と朱の水引
```
Create an original, modern Japanese sumi-e art print for the 2027 New Year, the Year of the Sheep (hinoto-hitsuji), to be displayed in a modern apartment entrance instead of traditional New Year decorations.

SUBJECT: A single ram standing still in profile, facing right, head held high, painted with a few confident strokes of deep black and grey sumi ink; the spiral horn is one bold continuous stroke with natural dry-brush streaks (kasure). Above and slightly in front of the ram, one single thin mizuhiki cord tied in an awaji-musubi knot, drawn as a fine, precise vermilion line, floating in the empty space like a New Year ornament. Original design only: do NOT depict any existing character or mascot, and do NOT imitate any specific artist's style.

COMPOSITION: Vertical poster. Place the ram in the lower-left area, occupying no more than 25–30% of the canvas. Place the small mizuhiki knot in the middle-right area, about 10% of the canvas width. Leave at least 70% of the canvas as empty negative space (Japanese "ma"). Keep all elements at least 8% from every edge for trimming to A-series proportions.

COLOR: Clean, cool off-white paper (#F7F5F0) with a very subtle washi fiber texture. Deep black ink (#1E1E1E) and one lighter grey ink. Vermilion (#C8553D) only for the mizuhiki cord and the seal; no other colors.

JAPANESE LETTERING: In the lower right, one small square vermilion (#C8553D) artist seal (rakkan), about 4% of the canvas width, with the single kanji 「未」 carved in seal-script (tensho) style, white lines on vermilion, slightly uneven hand-stamped edge. No other text, letters, numbers, signatures or watermarks.

MOOD: Crisp, dignified, fresh, and festive in a restrained way, like the cold clear air of New Year's morning.

OUTPUT: Highest available resolution (4K), 2:3 vertical. Crisp, print-ready flat artwork for an A4/A3 poster. No frame, no mockup, no room scene.
```

### 生成のコツ
- 羊が可愛く丸くなりすぎたら、「adult ram, not a cute lamb, no chibi style」を足す。
- 角の渦が細く弱くなったら、「single thick bold brushstroke for the horn」を足す。
- 印刷の注意(解像度、A判への切り出し、朱印の字形が崩れたときの対処)は初正月案の設計書と同じ。
